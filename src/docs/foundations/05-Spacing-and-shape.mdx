# Spacing and shape

## Spacing

Numbered steps (`space.1`–`5`) are layout spacing; named steps are component internals.

<TokenTable prefix="via.space." visual={SpaceBar} />

## Radius

Three steps: **control/field 20** (pills: buttons, fields, chips, pagination, side items),
**panel 12** (cards, panels, banners, dialogs, menus, toasts) and **inner 8** (rows and
items inside a card or menu, tooltips).

<TokenTable prefix="via.radius." visual={RadiusBox} />

## Sizes

Touch targets are 48 px (44 px minimum). Compact visuals such as 32 px buttons sit
inside a 44–48 px hit area.
